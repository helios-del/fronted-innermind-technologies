Domains & Impact

From inference to impact.

The Emotional Operating System is conceived as foundational infrastructure capable of empowering diverse human-centred environments. Select a category below to explore its applications.

Mental & Cognitive Health

Interfaces that recognize shifts in cognitive load, attention, and stress to provide supportive, non-intrusive accommodations.

  • Designed to recognize state trajectories rather than static discrete data points
  • Conceived to assist and enrich — never replace — qualified professional care
  • Built around dynamic session context rather than generic clinical averages

Personalised Digital Experiences

Adaptive software that tailors its presentation, density, and cadence according to real-time inferred psychological readiness.

  • Branches dynamically according to user state rather than static predetermined rules
  • Adjusts tone, pacing, and information complexity as fatigue or focus shifts
  • Treats personalisation as a continuous dialogue rather than a one-off profile setting

Human–AI Interaction

Enabling conversational agents and collaborative tools to maintain cognitive awareness across multi-turn workflows.

  • Maintains state continuity across complex problem-solving sessions
  • Adapts explanation granularity and collaboration style based on user receptivity
  • Operates on bidirectional context rather than isolated, transactional prompts

Future Intelligent Systems

A unified infrastructure enabling next-generation operating systems and spatial computing environments to understand human state.

  • Conceived as horizontal infrastructure on which diverse specialized systems can build
  • Engineered to generalize across operating environments without bespoke retraining
  • Maintained with ongoing exploration into multi-modal state synthesis
