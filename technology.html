Design Principles

How the architecture is built.

STREAMING

Continuous Adaptation

Conceived to update as context and inferred states evolve over the course of an active session.

MULTIVARIATE

No Single Score

Multiple dimensions are weighted together to prevent reductionist, one-size-fits-all adaptation.

ETHICAL

Human-Centred Limits

Designed with strict computational boundaries prioritizing human dignity and user control.
