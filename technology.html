Design principles

How the system is being built.

REAL-TIME

Continuous adaptation

Conceived to update as context and inferred state evolve, not just at the start of a session.

MULTIDIMENSIONAL

No single score

Multiple dimensions are considered together, avoiding one-size-fits-all personalisation.

RESPONSIBLE

Human-centred limits

Designed with the understanding that inferring human state carries real responsibility.
