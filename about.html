About Innermind

Technology, designed around the human being.

Innermind Technologies is an affective-computing company developing a foundational computational layer for understanding and responding to human cognitive and emotional states.

MISSION

Address Meaningful Human Problems

We are exploring how intelligent computing can become more adaptive, personalised, and supportive in the face of cognitive health challenges.

VISION

Architect Deep Cognition

Our vision is a computing ecosystem in which systems account for the richness of human psychological states instead of treating every interaction as static.

APPROACH

Research-Led & Human-Centric

We combine affective computing, statistical inference, and adaptive systems with a rigorous commitment to responsible technology design.

We're not trying to make software louder or faster.
We're trying to make it more aware.
Research Philosophy

An interdisciplinary starting point.

Interpreting human state responsibly sits at the intersection of affective computing, cognitive psychology, and inference architectures. We treat that intersection as an active research discipline — maintaining transparency about what is conceptual, what is currently under development, and what remains an open scientific challenge.

Long-Term Ambition

A foundational layer, not a single product.

Our long-term ambition is for the Emotional Operating System to operate as infrastructure — a foundational layer that human-centred applications across healthcare, adaptive learning, and intelligent collaboration can build upon.

How We Work

Principles we hold ourselves to.

01

Human-Centred First

Every architectural decision is evaluated based on whether it genuinely serves the wellbeing and agency of the individual user.

02

Responsible by Default

Inferring psychological states carries significant responsibility. Privacy, security, and consent are foundational architectural constraints.

03

Honest About the Frontier

We characterize our technology rigorously — clearly distinguishing between established capabilities and active frontiers.
